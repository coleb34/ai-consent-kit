Privacy

We collect only what checkout needs: your name, email address, state, license type and practice name, plus payment details, which Stripe processes (we never see your card number). We use this to deliver your kit and its updates, to answer support requests, and for accounting. We do not sell or share it except with the services that run checkout and email.

The waitlist is our newsletter's sign-up on Buttondown; you can unsubscribe from any email.

Never send us client information. The kit does not need it and we do not want it.

To ask for your data to be deleted, write to therapyaiconsent@gmail.com.

Not legal advice. The kit is AI-prepared templates and general information, checked against published sources. It does not create an attorney-client relationship. Have an attorney licensed in your state review the documents before you rely on them.
