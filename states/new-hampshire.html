AI laws to know in New Hampshire

LawStatusEffectiveWhat it covers
RSA 507:8-k (Laws 2025, ch. 270), AI chat programs and childrenIn effectJanuary 1, 2026Owners and operators of AI chat programs are liable when they knowingly direct to a child a communication intended to encourage sexually explicit conduct, illegal drug or alcohol use, self-harm or suicide, or violence; the attorney general enforces it.

What this means for your practice

  • Get the consent of all parties before recording a session: New Hampshire law makes it a crime to wilfully intercept a telecommunication or oral communication without the consent of all parties to the communication, unless the chapter provides otherwise. Source

Good practice everywhere, whatever your state requires

  • Written consent from every adult participant before recording or transcribing a session.
  • A HIPAA business associate agreement for any AI tool that receives client information.
  • A clinician reviews every AI-assisted note.
  • AI never delivers therapy or makes clinical decisions.
