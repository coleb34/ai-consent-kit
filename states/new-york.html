AI laws to know in New York

LawStatusEffectiveWhat it covers
General Business Law art. 47 (2025), AI companion modelsIn effectNovember 5, 2025Operators of AI companions must have a protocol for detecting and addressing suicidal ideation or self-harm with crisis referrals, and must notify users at the start of an interaction and at least every three hours that they are not communicating with a human.
S 9051-B (Laws of 2026, ch. 368; General Business Law art. 48), unsafe AI companion features for minorsEnacted, not yet in effectJanuary 1, 2027Makes it unlawful for an operator to provide unsafe AI companion features to a user unless the operator has determined the user is not a minor; the attorney general enforces it with civil penalties of up to $25,000 per violation.

Good practice everywhere, whatever your state requires

  • Written consent from every adult participant before recording or transcribing a session.
  • A HIPAA business associate agreement for any AI tool that receives client information.
  • A clinician reviews every AI-assisted note.
  • AI never delivers therapy or makes clinical decisions.
