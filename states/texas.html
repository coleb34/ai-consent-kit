AI laws to know in Texas

LawStatusEffectiveWhat it covers
HB 149 (2025), Texas Responsible Artificial Intelligence Governance Act (Bus. & Com. Code ch. 552)In effectJanuary 1, 2026Requires the provider of a health care service or treatment to disclose when an AI system is used in relation to it, and bars developing or deploying AI in a manner that intentionally aims to incite or encourage self-harm, harm to another person or criminal activity.
SB 1188 (2025), electronic health records and AI (Health & Safety Code ch. 183)In effectSeptember 1, 2025Allows health care practitioners to use AI for diagnostic purposes within their scope if they review AI-created records, requires them to disclose that use to patients, and requires covered entities to keep electronic health records physically in the United States.

Good practice everywhere, whatever your state requires

  • Written consent from every adult participant before recording or transcribing a session.
  • A HIPAA business associate agreement for any AI tool that receives client information.
  • A clinician reviews every AI-assisted note.
  • AI never delivers therapy or makes clinical decisions.
