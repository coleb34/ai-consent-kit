AI laws to know in Colorado

LawStatusEffectiveWhat it covers
HB26-1139 Use of Artificial Intelligence in Health Care (ch. 325)Enacted, not yet in effectJanuary 1, 2027Sets requirements for carriers and other entities that use AI systems for utilization review, requires human review before a medical-necessity denial, and bars carriers and state medical assistance payers from paying for psychotherapy services conducted by an AI system.
HB26-1195 Psychotherapy Artificial Intelligence Restrictions (ch. 358)In effectAugust 12, 2026Restricts how regulated psychotherapy professionals may use AI systems with clients and requires advance disclosure and written informed consent before AI records or transcribes a session.
HB26-1263 Conversational Artificial Intelligence Service Operator Requirements (ch. 208)In effectAugust 12, 2026 (ch. 208); operator duties start Jan 1, 2027Sets requirements for operators of publicly available conversational AI services, including an AI disclosure, protections for minors, a protocol for prompts about suicidal ideation or self-harm, and a ban on stating that outputs are provided by or equivalent to licensed professionals.
SB26-189 Automated Decision-Making Technology (ch. 131)In effectMay 14, 2026 (ch. 131); developer duties start Jan 1, 2027Repeals and reenacts Senate Bill 24-205 with requirements for the use of automated decision-making technology in consequential decisions, including decisions about health-care services.

What this means for your practice

  • HB26-1195 defines regulated professionals as individuals lawfully permitted to provide psychotherapy services in the state. (effective 08/12/2026) Source
  • If you are lawfully permitted to provide psychotherapy services in the state, you are a regulated professional under HB26-1195 (Psychotherapy Artificial Intelligence Restrictions, effective 08/12/2026). (effective 08/12/2026) Source

Good practice everywhere, whatever your state requires

  • Written consent from every adult participant before recording or transcribing a session.
  • A HIPAA business associate agreement for any AI tool that receives client information.
  • A clinician reviews every AI-assisted note.
  • AI never delivers therapy or makes clinical decisions.
