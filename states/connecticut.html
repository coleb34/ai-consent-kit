AI laws to know in Connecticut

LawStatusEffectiveWhat it covers
SB 5 (Public Act 26-15), sections 4 to 6: artificial intelligence companionsEnacted, not yet in effectJanuary 1, 2027Operators may not provide an AI companion without an evidence-based protocol to detect expressions of suicide, self-harm or violence risk and refer users to mental health resources, with added limits for users under 18, including on offering mental health services.

Good practice everywhere, whatever your state requires

  • Written consent from every adult participant before recording or transcribing a session.
  • A HIPAA business associate agreement for any AI tool that receives client information.
  • A clinician reviews every AI-assisted note.
  • AI never delivers therapy or makes clinical decisions.
