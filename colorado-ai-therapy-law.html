What it requires of your practice

  • HB26-1195 defines regulated professionals as individuals lawfully permitted to provide psychotherapy services in the state. (effective 08/12/2026) Source
  • If you are lawfully permitted to provide psychotherapy services in the state, you are a regulated professional under HB26-1195 (Psychotherapy Artificial Intelligence Restrictions, effective 08/12/2026). (effective 08/12/2026) Source

Sample Practice · Colorado

This form asks for your permission to use an artificial intelligence (AI) documentation tool in your care. It is separate from your general consent to treatment. You can say no, and saying no will not affect your care in any way.

What the tool does

What the tool does not do

  • It does not provide therapy, make treatment decisions, diagnose, or assess risk. Your clinician makes all clinical decisions.
  • Your clinician reviews and edits every note before it becomes part of your record.
  • Sample Practice uses only tools covered by a business associate agreement under HIPAA, or that do not receive identifiable health information.

Everyone in the session

If anyone else takes part in a session (for example a partner or family member), each adult present must also agree before any recording begins. For a minor, a parent or legal guardian signs as required by law.

Your choices

  • You may decline. Your care, scheduling and fees will not change.
  • You may withdraw consent at any time, in writing or by telling your clinician. Withdrawal applies from that point on. Ask your clinician what happens to recordings already made.
  • You may ask questions about the tool at any time.
